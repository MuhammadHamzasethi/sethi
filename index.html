05 / FAQ

Questions
clients ask.

Where are you based?

Our team is based in Pakistan and works US hours, 9am to 6pm Eastern, so we're available during your working day. We work with clients in the US, UK, Europe, Australia and Asia.

How does payment work?

Half before we start and half when you approve the finished work. Larger projects are split into milestones. You can also hire us through Upwork or Fiverr.

What if I don't like the design?

You approve the design before we build anything, and two rounds of changes are included in every price.

Can you fix a site someone else built?

Yes. Send us the link and what's wrong, and we'll tell you the cause and the price for free.

Who owns the website?

You do. The code, the content and the domain are yours from day one.
